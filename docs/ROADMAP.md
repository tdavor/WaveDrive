# WaveDrive
Your road. Your radio.

Version target: 1.0.0

- Flutter UI, English, Swedish, Croatian.
- Radio Browser API, country and genre browsing, search, favorites, recent stations, custom stream URLs.
- Android Media3 background playback and Android Auto media library.
- iOS AVPlayer and CarPlay audio integration subject to Apple entitlement.
- Local storage; no account or backend required.
- APK-only test distribution via GitHub Releases. No AAB or Play submission until explicitly requested.
- Navy, cyan and white visual identity.
- Validate stream errors, interruptions, reconnection and vehicle controls before release.
