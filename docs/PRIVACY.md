## English

WaveDrive is an internet radio player. No account or registration is required. We do not operate a user account database, use an advertising SDK, or integrate analytics or crash-reporting services in the current release.

**Information stored on your device.** Your favorites, recent stations, language and playback preferences, and cached station images are stored locally for app functionality. The app does not intentionally upload these lists or preferences to the developer. Android system backup may handle app data according to your device and Google account settings.

**Network connections and recipients.** WaveDrive connects to the Radio Browser public directory to retrieve stations. Search terms, selected country or genre, IP address and technical request information can be received by that directory. Radio streams and station images are fetched directly from independent broadcasters and image hosts. Those providers can receive your IP address, requested stream or image, and technical connection information; they may log requests, infer approximate location from the IP address, or apply their own retention and privacy practices. WaveDrive does not control these providers, their broadcasts, or advertising embedded in broadcasts. These connections are used to deliver directory searches, audio and artwork. See [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info/) and the relevant broadcaster's privacy information. WaveDrive does not sell personal information or use it for developer-controlled advertising or profiling.

**Android Auto and external services.** Android Auto receives media information such as station, artwork and current track to display and control playback. Google processes information according to its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The optional Buy Me a Coffee link opens an external service subject to its own [privacy policy](https://buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy); any donations or payment details are handled by that service, not by WaveDrive. Google Play manages updates for the Play version; the separate GitHub APK edition checks GitHub for updates.

**Security and retention.** Directory requests use HTTPS. Individual radio streams or image hosts may use HTTP or HTTPS, so not every connection is encrypted. Local settings and cached files remain on your device until removed. You can delete them through Android Settings → Apps → WaveDrive → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app; Android backups may be managed separately. Independent service providers determine their own log retention. If you contact the developer by email, your email and message are used to respond and retained as needed to resolve your inquiry or meet applicable obligations.

**Your choices and rights.** You can stop streaming, avoid external links, clear local app data, or contact the developer to request access, correction or deletion of information the developer holds, where applicable. You may also contact the relevant third-party provider regarding its records. Where applicable, you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. The app is a general internet radio player, not designed specifically for children; third-party live broadcasts may contain mature content.

Updates to this policy will be published on this page. Contact the developer at the email above with privacy questions.

## Svenska — Integritetspolicy

WaveDrive är en internetradioapp utan krav på konto. Utvecklaren använder ingen egen användardatabas, annons-SDK eller integrerad analys- eller kraschrapporteringstjänst i denna version. Favoriter, senaste stationer, språk, uppspelningsinställningar och cachelagrade bilder sparas lokalt. Androids säkerhetskopiering kan hantera appdata enligt dina enhetsinställningar.

Sökningar skickas till den offentliga katalogen Radio Browser, som kan ta emot sökord, valt land eller genre, IP-adress och teknisk anslutningsinformation. Ljudströmmar och bilder hämtas direkt från oberoende radiostationer och bildvärdar. De kan ta emot och logga IP-adress, efterfrågat innehåll och tekniska uppgifter, samt härleda ungefärlig plats från IP-adressen. Deras egna integritetsregler och lagringstider gäller. Utvecklaren styr inte externa sändningar eller reklam i dem och säljer inte personuppgifter eller använder dem för egen reklamprofilering.

Android Auto får medieinformation för uppspelning och visning. Google hanterar sina tjänster enligt sin integritetspolicy. Den frivilliga länken till Buy Me a Coffee öppnar en extern tjänst som hanterar eventuella betalningar. Google Play hanterar uppdateringar av Play-versionen; den separata GitHub-versionen söker uppdateringar via GitHub.

Katalogen använder HTTPS, men externa strömmar och bilder kan använda HTTP. Lokala uppgifter kan tas bort via Androids appinställningar genom att rensa lagringen eller avinstallera appen. Säkerhetskopior kan behöva hanteras separat. E-post till utvecklaren används för att besvara frågor och sparas så länge det behövs för ärendet eller rättsliga skyldigheter. Kontakta utvecklaren för frågor eller för att begära tillgång, rättelse eller radering av uppgifter som utvecklaren har, i tillämpliga fall. Du kan även vända dig till respektive extern tjänst och behörig tillsynsmyndighet. Appen är inte särskilt utformad för barn; externa radiosändningar kan innehålla vuxeninnehåll.

## Hrvatski — Pravila privatnosti

WaveDrive je aplikacija za internetski radio bez obvezne registracije. U ovoj verziji razvojni programer ne koristi vlastitu bazu korisničkih računa, oglasni SDK ni integrirane usluge analitike ili izvještavanja o rušenju aplikacije. Omiljene i nedavno slušane stanice, jezik, postavke reprodukcije i predmemorirane slike spremaju se lokalno. Androidova sigurnosna kopija može obrađivati podatke prema postavkama uređaja.

Upiti se šalju javnom katalogu Radio Browser, koji može primiti pojmove pretraživanja, odabranu državu ili žanr, IP adresu i tehničke podatke o vezi. Zvuk i slike dohvaćaju se izravno od neovisnih radijskih postaja i poslužitelja slika. Oni mogu primiti i bilježiti IP adresu, traženi sadržaj i tehničke podatke te iz IP adrese procijeniti približnu lokaciju. Primjenjuju se njihova pravila privatnosti i rokovi čuvanja podataka. WaveDrive ne kontrolira vanjski sadržaj ni oglase u programu postaja, ne prodaje osobne podatke i ne koristi ih za vlastito oglašavanje ili profiliranje.

Android Auto prima medijske informacije radi prikaza i upravljanja reprodukcijom. Google obrađuje podatke svojih usluga prema vlastitim pravilima. Dobrovoljna poveznica Buy Me a Coffee otvara vanjsku uslugu koja obrađuje eventualne uplate. Google Play upravlja nadogradnjama Play verzije, a zasebna GitHub verzija provjerava nadogradnje preko GitHuba.

Katalog koristi HTTPS, ali vanjski streamovi i slike mogu koristiti HTTP. Lokalne podatke možeš ukloniti brisanjem pohrane aplikacije u Android postavkama ili deinstalacijom; sigurnosnim kopijama upravlja se zasebno. Poruke e-pošte razvojnom programeru služe za odgovor na upite i čuvaju se koliko je potrebno za rješavanje upita ili zakonske obveze. Za pitanja ili zahtjev za pristup, ispravak ili brisanje podataka koje razvojni programer posjeduje, obrati se na navedenu adresu. Možeš se obratiti i vanjskim pružateljima usluga te nadležnom tijelu za zaštitu podataka. Aplikacija nije posebno namijenjena djeci; vanjski radijski program može sadržavati sadržaj za odrasle.
